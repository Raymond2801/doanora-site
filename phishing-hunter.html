PHISHING HUNTER

THREE QUICK CHECKS

  1. Who really sent it? Read the address, not just the name beside it.
  2. Where does the link go? Check the real address before you click, and use https.
  3. What does it want? Urgency, secrecy, passwords, codes, gift cards and quick payments are red flags.
